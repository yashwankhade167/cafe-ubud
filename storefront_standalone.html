Our Story

Born from a dream
of Balinese calm.

We built Cafe Ubud not to open another café — but to transplant a feeling. The soft mist over Ubud's rice paddies. The sound of a bamboo chime. The first sip of coffee in a jungle resort at golden hour.

In Amravati, we created a portal. Every detail — the aroma, the light, the textures — is chosen to make you forget you're 5,000 kilometres from Bali.

Read the Full Story
01
Why Bali?

Bali doesn't just refresh — it transforms.

The island taught us that the greatest luxury is time — time to breathe, taste, and be present. We brought that philosophy home.

02
The Coffee Philosophy

Single-origin beans. Infinite ritual.

We source directly from small-lot farms in Bali's Kintamani highlands. Every bean is traceable to a family, a harvest, a story.

03
The Café as Art

A space designed to slow you down.

Bamboo textures, living plants, warm candlelight, and ambient rain sounds from Ubud's rice terraces. This is not décor — it's atmosphere, engineered for peace.
